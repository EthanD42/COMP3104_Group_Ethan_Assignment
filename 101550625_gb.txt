George Brown College (GBC) - Information

George Brown College is a public college located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
It was founded in 1967 and is named after George Brown, a Father of
Confederation and founder of the Toronto Globe newspaper.

Key Facts:
- Campuses: St. James Campus, Casa Loma Campus, Waterfront Campus,
  and Sun Life Financial Centre
- Programs: Offers over 170 full-time programs across areas including
  business, health sciences, technology, design, and hospitality
- Student Population: Approximately 30,000+ full-time students
- 

Notable Features:
- Strong ties to Toronto's business and tech community
- Applied research opportunities for students
- Diverse student body from over 100 countries
-
